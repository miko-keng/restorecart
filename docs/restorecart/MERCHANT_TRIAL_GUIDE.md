# Merchant trial protocol (no results claimed)

Recruit three consenting small sellers who can share non-sensitive example records. Do not send their data to Gemini without explaining the configured provider and obtaining their agreement. Use sandbox payments and isolated test stores only.

## Trial tasks

1. Give the seller a fresh workspace and their sample CSV/PDF/photos. Ask them to reconstruct and publish three products without coaching.
2. Include one deliberately conflicting price or ambiguous variant/photo. Observe whether they know what to correct and why publication is blocked.
3. Ask a second tester to find a product, save a basket and complete PayPal sandbox checkout. Seller marks ready; buyer checks the receipt; seller marks collected.
4. Stop new orders, download the report, compare and apply file recovery. Repeat and verify zero duplicate changes.
5. Optional connected WooCommerce test: return the sale and inspect destination orders and stock. Do not claim automatic platform recovery if only local JSON was tested.
6. Save a shop backup, restore as a fresh draft and explain why stock must be recounted and historical payments are not copied.
